Privacy Policy — Christmas Color by Number

Last updated: 30 September 2026

In short: no account, no server, no location. Your pictures and progress stay on your phone. The only outside service is Google AdMob, which shows the ads that keep the app free.

1. What the app stores

  • Which squares you have painted, in which order (for the timelapse), which pictures you unlocked, and how many magic wands you have.
  • All of it is stored in the app's own storage on your device. Nothing is sent to us: we run no server and have no account system.
  • The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

2. Sharing a picture

When you tap Share, the app creates an image of your finished picture on the device and hands it to Android's share menu. It goes only to the app or person you choose there.

3. Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob (banners, full-screen ads between pictures, and optional videos that unlock pictures or give magic wands). To serve ads, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, technical information about the device, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We give advertisers no personal data.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent screen asks for your choice about personalised ads before any ad is shown. If you decline, you see non-personalised ads and the app stays fully usable. You can change your choice at any time from the menu (⋮) → Privacy options in the gallery.

Details of Google's processing: Google's privacy policy.

4. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app erases everything it stored. Since we hold nothing about you, there is nothing to ask us to delete.

5. Children

The app is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

We hold no personal data about you, so your rights are exercised on your device: changing your ad consent or uninstalling. For data Google processes as a controller, please contact Google.

7. Changes and contact

Changes will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: chocham.123@gmail.com
